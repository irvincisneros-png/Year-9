The name is NOT "Year": it is `Year 9 Topic Booklet - Diseases.pdf` (the space-separated listing in the brief truncated it). `file` says: PDF document, version 1.7, 46 pages. Nothing references it (grep for "uploads" and "Booklet" in the repo finds nothing); the app only uses `images/raw-*.jpg` (pages extracted from it) and `home.jsx` calls raw-00.jpg "topic booklet cover".
